Sun-Dried tomato Pasta for Dinner time

this is a small recipe to prepare when you are in a rush if you enjoy traditional tomato flavor

Ingredients and Instructions

Pasta, butter, garlic, tomato sauce, fresh tomatoes, salt, pepper Cook the pasta until all good to taste. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a skillet. Sauté the garlic for a minute. Stir in the tomato paste, tomato sauce, cream, and Italian seasoning until smooth.
