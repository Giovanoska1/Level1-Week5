The Art of Smoky Eyes

Master the iconic smoky eye look with our easy-to-follow activity. This timeless style adds depth and drama, perfect for an evening out or any occasion where you want to make a bold statement. Let's dive into the steps to achieve that perfect smoky eye.

Smoky Effect:

Apply a dark shadow (like charcoal or dark brown) along your eyelid and into the crease. Blend upwards and outwards. The key is blending; you want no harsh lines.
